Frequently Asked Questions

The app blocks aren't working after I finish meditating.

Go to Settings > Screen Time on your device and confirm that Gateles has Family Controls access. If blocks persist, force-quit the app and reopen it to trigger a sync.

The camera isn't detecting my pose.

Make sure you're seated upright with your face and upper body visible in the frame, in a well-lit environment. If the issue persists, go to iOS Settings > Gateles and confirm camera access is enabled.

My HealthKit meditation session isn't being recognized.

Ensure the session was logged as a Mindfulness or Meditation type in Apple Health and that it started after you opened Gateles. Go to iOS Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Gateles and confirm read access is granted.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple. Go to iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Gateles and tap Cancel Subscription.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email us at helpgateles@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" and we'll process the request within 30 days.
